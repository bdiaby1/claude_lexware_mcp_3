# SHORTS BRIEF — ISTANBUL (Tahsin)

Source: pasted by Benjamin in chat, 2026-09-11, verbatim. His words are the master.

**3 shorts. Each one works standalone — no context from the long-form needed.**
Format: vertical 9:16 · 20–30 sec each · YouTube Shorts + Instagram Reels + TikTok, same file.

---

## SHORT #1 — CATS vs PEOPLE

**NO VOICEOVER.** Music, labels and cuts only — the viewer draws the comparison himself. Also travels better internationally without spoken language.

**STRUCTURE**

Opening (2 sec): full-screen text — **"How Istanbul treats CATS vs PEOPLE"** — then it disappears.

After that only small labels top-left, alternating: **CAT:** / **HUMAN:**

**CLIP ORDER** — always cat first, human as the punch:

1. **CAT:** cat sitting on the Starbucks couch → **HUMAN:** Tahsin at the bins
2. **CAT:** someone feeding a street cat → **HUMAN:** Tahsin pulling food out of the trash
3. **CAT:** cats lying next to the soldier (best shot) → **HUMAN:** Tahsin pulling the cart between moving cars
4. **CAT:** cat sleeping somewhere warm → **HUMAN:** his blanket on the empty floor
5. **END:** his quote if we have it — *"Yes. That's true."* Or text card: **He said yes. The cats are treated better.**

**TIMING:** every clip 1.5–2 seconds, hard cuts on the beat. No fades, no transitions.

**MUSIC:** trending sound from the CapCut library, emotional/melancholic (sad piano, emotional violin, slowed reverb). Must have a clear beat drop — place the drop on clip 3 (soldier/cart). Use in-app audio, not imported.

**TEXT:** bold white, thin black outline, top-left, small. Labels stay on screen while the clip runs, no animation.

**ENDING:** hold the final frame 1 second with the quote. No subscribe screen. Handle small in the corner throughout.

## SHORT #2 — THE SUPERMARKET

**WITH VOICEOVER — two lines only.**

**VO line 1** (over the first clips):
> *This is the trash of Istanbul's rich neighborhood. He calls it his supermarket.*

Then just clips, one after another, each 1.5 sec, with a small label naming what comes out:

**10 fresh eggs** → **cream cheese, sealed** → **salami, sealed** → **Burberry shirt** → **gold chain** → **two vapes — both work**

**VO line 2** (over the last shot, alone, nothing after it):
> *None of it was opened. Not one thing.*

**End card:** *One neighborhood sleeps on the floor. The next one throws away breakfast.*

**MUSIC:** rising tension, drop on the gold chain. Duck the music under both VO lines.

**VO TONE:** stunned but quiet. Not outraged — just describing what he saw. Benjamin records both lines in two versions (calm / slightly faster) so the editor can match the music.

*Why it works standalone: pure fascination, no pity. People stay to see what comes out next — that's the loop.*

## SHORT #3 — THE MATH

**NO VOICEOVER.** The numbers build the tension by themselves — a voice would break the pace.

**Opening:** Benjamin at the coffee terrace, Hagia Sophia in the background. Text: *This coffee: 500 lira.*

Then: the depot, the scale, the calculator, the money in his hand. Text appears step by step:

*67 kilos* → *minus 23 for the cart* → *44 kilos* → *one full night* → **389 lira**

**Final card:** *His whole night can't buy my coffee. 111 lira short.*

**MUSIC:** countdown feel, hard stop on the final number.

*Why it works: numbers going down while the viewer waits for the punchline. Nobody swipes before they see the total.*

---

## GENERAL RULES FOR ALL THREE

- Every short needs **one loop, one payoff, one hit at the end** — none of them should feel like a trailer.
- Hard cuts only. No transitions, no zooms, no effects.
- Text always small and top-left, never centered, never big.
- No "subscribe" end screen — handle stays small in the corner throughout.
- Blur any faces at the depot. No wide shots that identify the location.
