a sequence of otherwise real footage. Your brand rests on "it's really him" and real

call fake, and it would land on the strongest moment of the video.
Options, your call: leave it (it is normal documentary grammar), or make it unmistakably
a reconstruction (grade, speed, a one-word title), or carry Tahsin's own telling over
the real street instead. Not deleting anything — flagging it next to the shot.
