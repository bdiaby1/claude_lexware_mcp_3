## Format rules established with him (29 Sep)
- **Story = 15s segments.** IG auto-splits; a 40s story becomes 3 pieces and viewers
  drop at each seam. A joke/BTS clip: cut to the 15s where the punch sits, OR post as
  a Reel (no 15s limit, gets discovery, permanent). Story = the moment; Reel = discovery.
- **No explaining caption on a self-running clip** — it kills the spontaneity. Max a
  one-word context sticker. Never a self-deprecating text over it (the figure he's
  building is dry/self-ironic, not self-diminishing — "every girl's nightmare" killed).
- Verdict rule: worth-seeing + a human → Reel · "ich war hier", nice but incidental →
  Story · he himself hesitates → nothing.
