## PICK 1 — "Low Income Families in Europe's richest city (Zürich)"  [ClickUp 86akkwpr5]
Defines the WHERE. The invisible in the RICHEST city on earth = the thesis made universal,
and it kills the poverty-tourism/colonial read ("rich guy visits poor country"). Nobody
owns this. Zürich is the gift.
- Feasibility: needs family access → use the germany-subjects sourcing method (press/vetted
  leads, not raw virals; verify before filming). If families are slow, start with individual
  overlooked people in Zürich — same thesis. Kids → §6, never identifiable.

## PICK 2 — "Homeless man plans the best day of my life / I hand over my bank card"  [ClickUp 86aj1mq19]
Defines the HOW. Role reversal: the invisible person is the AUTHORITY on a good life, he's
the student. Respect + learning, not charity or pity. Warm/hopeful (the heart-not-fear
pivot). A repeatable FORMAT (any city, one human each). "You hand over the definition of a
good life."
- Feasibility: fastest to shoot in Zürich — one willing person, one day, big payoff. Clean
  consent, no kids.

Why both: #1 = where (invisibility everywhere, even in wealth), #2 = how (the invisible as
teacher, dignity not pity). Together: "I go to the richest places and let the people
everyone walks past teach me what a good life is."
