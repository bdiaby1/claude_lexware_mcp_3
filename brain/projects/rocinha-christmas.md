YouTube: published 27 Dec 2025, 237 views (older, pre-growth). Rocinha, Rio, Christmas Day.
Repurposed as an IG carousel Oct 2026.

## Thesis (his, from the transcript)
He runs an agency / works with money daily — could afford any Christmas. Leaves the
bubble → Rocinha. Core question: "who is Christmas actually built for?" Christmas = not
celebration but PRESSURE, a culture designed around spending; the poorest aren't even in
the picture. Rocinha sits between two ultra-rich neighbourhoods. 15-yr-olds with rifles
(some LV-design, ammo bags). The gut-punch: a kid got a perfume + t-shirt and "doesn't
remember what he got." Faith layer (woven, §1): in Islam the celebration is incomplete
while someone is hungry — Zakat, structural. Close: "money is a tool — not to upgrade
your life but to unlock small dreams for others. Maybe Christmas doesn't need more gifts.
Maybe it needs more honesty."
