# GERMANY SUBJECTS — sourcing real stories for the next documentary

Goal: find real German people/families in hardship whose story Benjamin can tell with
depth and dignity (the "invisible made visible" in Germany — his engine, §2). TikTok is
the raw source; his value-add is turning a 60s raw clip into a 9-min dignified film.

## SOURCING METHOD (what actually works from a Claude Code session, verified 2026-10-01)
- **Cannot** scrape TikTok hashtag feeds directly (app/login/JS, blocks servers like YT).
- **Can** WebSearch for stories that are PRESS-COVERED or on VETTED FUNDRAISING PLATFORMS.
  These are *safer* leads: journalists/platforms already did a first vetting (Fallstrick 1),
  and the people chose visibility. Treat every hit as a LEAD TO VERIFY, never a confirmed
  subject.
- Search in German; sort/think "recent" (fresh = no other creator on it yet).
- His own TikTok method (for when he searches the app): #obdachlos #wohnungslos
  #armutdeutschland #alleinerziehend + city; sort "this week"; READ COMMENTS (real stories
  draw local reactions "ich kenne die Gegend/ich hab gespendet"; fakes draw generic praise
  or authenticity fights); cross-check consistency across the person's videos + any local
  press/Verein mention before contacting.
