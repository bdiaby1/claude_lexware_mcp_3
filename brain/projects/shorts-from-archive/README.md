wrong operating system: the doc runs on OUTRAGE ("Wut, Heuchelei, 'schau wie krank diese
Welt ist'") and ends every short on a narrated verdict — his brand runs on WARMTH and
"the contrast does the judging" (MEMORY §1b, §2.7). Keep the strategy, rewrite every line.

   a verdict-question ("Is this world broken or are we blind?", "What's wrong with us?",
   "always pure narcissism?") — §2.7 forbids the narrated verdict. Ever.

   On his profile the 500-Lira coffee short is the LOSER (396 views) vs Tahsin's face
   (1,516) and the cats concept (1,088) — `../instagram-highlights/profile_state.md`.

3. **Not his voice (§4).** "Wohlstands-Simulation", "insulated bubble", "moral
   validation", "hustle culture", "eiskalt", "I HATE THIS CLIP" — AI-smooth drama lines.
   Shorts VO rule: deadpan over insane footage; a dramatic voice = try-hard.
4. **Fabrication creep (§6).** The doc inflates his material in every pass — see flags.
