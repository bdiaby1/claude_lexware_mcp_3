# HIS LINES — the voice corpus (verbatim from his own scripts in this brain)

Why this file exists: on 9 Oct three Shorts drafts failed in a row because Claude wrote from
RULES ("be sharp", "be warm") instead of from HIS SENTENCES. He said: "die Sprache muss
wirklich meine Sprache sein … so spreche ich einfach nicht." Before writing ANY line for him,
read these. Every new line must pass: could it sit between two of these without a seam?

## Patterns (how he builds a sentence)
- **Introduce a human in fragments:** "That's Tahsin. Over sixty. Every night he pulls this
  cart through Istanbul. Buses. Cars. Knives." / "Kamara. Kindergarten teacher. Twenty-eight
  years in Germany. From Guinea — my father's country."
- **Then put himself in it, one line:** "Tonight I pulled it with him." / "Yesterday, for the
  first time, I stopped."
- **Numbers as a sentence, no adjectives:** "This morning I paid 500 lira for one coffee.
  Tonight we find out if his whole night can buy it." / "The whole night: 389 lira." /
  "His whole night can't buy my coffee. It's 111 lira short." / "This toilet costs the city
  over fifty thousand euros a year. Right now, someone is injecting inside."
- **Contrast as two flat statements:** "One neighborhood sleeps on the floor. The next one
  throws away breakfast." / "Friday morning, these men kneel in the mosque. In the afternoon,
  some of them sell drugs in this park. Same men. Same day."
- **Admit the small human thing, dry:** "I won't lie. My stomach had opinions. Until the
  first bite." / "He walked me home. I was limping. He wasn't." / "Because I'm a little crazy."
- **The seeing line (channel DNA):** "They were always there. I just never looked." /
  "I walked past this park a hundred times. Now I can't unsee what I saw."
- **The hero gets the last word, often his own clip:** "He said he didn't see it. That's why
  he's here." / "If they had good jobs — I believe they would never come here again."
- **Close with a sentence, not a question (long-form):** "What counts is how the story ends." /
  "The soul doesn't get clean from water. It gets clean from what you do." / "I sit with the
  people everyone walks past. This week, one of them cooked for me."
- **Shorts: statement stack → one question that includes him:** "This is a homeless man. In my
  bathrobe. In my penthouse. … He gave me his cardboard. I gave him my home. The stoics called
  it practicing poverty. The prophets sat with the poor. So what about us?"
- **Humour is dry and aimed at himself or the situation, never at the person:** "No wonder
  someone tries to rob you at night, walking around in a 3,000 lira shirt. He laughed for a
  minute." / "Oh no, not again." (the bus)

## What he never says
Adjective-stacks ("mediocre", "lukewarm", "insulated bubble"), insults to a place or group,
"we as a society", "hustle culture", poetic metaphors, a lecture. He states, he doesn't argue.
