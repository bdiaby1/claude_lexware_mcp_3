# GÖRLITZER PARK — FINAL VOICEOVER SHEET

Record at home, clean audio, indoors. Tone: tired, honest, flat — the footage carries the drama, the voice stays out of the way. Pauses instead of volume. Go quieter on heavy lines.
Best: record AFTER Michael's rough cut, speaking onto the pictures.
One block = one take. Labels are for orientation only — speak only the text.

---

**1 · [over 3D map zoom → the park]**

Last year, this park had more registered crimes than the next eight parks of Berlin combined.

---

**2 · [Kotti street life]**

This is Kreuzberg. Maybe the only place on earth where the woman in niqab, the drug addict, the trans person and the drug dealer share the same park bench.

---

**3 · [mosque interior, Friday prayer]**

Friday morning, these men kneel in the mosque. In the afternoon, some of them sell drugs in this park — strictly forbidden by the faith they just prayed in. Same men. Same day.

---

**4 · [the one-take run mosque → park]**

The mosque and the park — watch how close they are.

---

**5 · [man walks into the frame]**

It's difficult to film here. Even my standing camera gets questioned.

---

**6 · [refusals montage]**

Most don't want the camera. Some get aggressive. One said: I'll answer anything — but not on film.

---

**7 · [Fixpunkt buses]**

They even build buses, so addicts can inject more safely.

---

**8 · [people walking into the bushes]**

If you wonder why people keep walking into the trees — the bushes are the warehouse. The whole park's supply is stored in there.

---

**9 · [the marijuana packets in hand]**

Which, by the way, is theoretically legal in Germany under certain circumstances. But of course I don't consume or have anything to do with it.

---

**10 · [barber, cook, freestyle — the park flips]**

But then you sit a while. And the park flips. A barber cuts hair on a rock. A man cooks rice for everyone — African street food. First there's distance. Then a little trust — and suddenly it's like a restaurant. Alive.

---

**11 · [first shot of Kamara, blurred]**

For two days, everyone said no. Then one man sat down. Kamara. Kindergarten teacher. Twenty-eight years in Germany. From Guinea — my father's country.

---

**12 · [3D route map Gambia → Sahara → Libya → sea → Berlin]**

Many of these men are Gambia's sons. For twenty-two years, one dictator ruled there. Survivors say he forced the most beautiful girls into his palace — before they were allowed to marry. So the young men fled. And their families married them first — so they would come back. Now they're here. Not allowed to work. With a family waiting for money every week.

---

**13 · [the mosque lit blue, police operation]**

While we filmed, two houses from the mosque: two hundred police officers. Twenty cars.
