## DAY 1 — KOTTBUSSER TOR + THE MOSQUE

### The mosque
- Spent the first day in the mosque — the mosque directly beside the park.
- Filmed the interior, especially the ablution place where people clean themselves. Very beautiful. The prayer hall inside as well.
- Filmed the Friday prayer — beautiful.
- Filmed Africans cleaning themselves inside the mosque.
- Filmed Africans standing outside the mosque, right in front of the door — walking, giving each other handshakes, having fun — before they go to the park.
- [ADD] This is the same image as the KL mosque moment in the swap video (homeless washing in the mosque — "a place built to clean your soul"). Second time the brand's strongest motif appears on its own. The mosque here is the SOUL pole of the video; the park is the LOST pole. Everything cuts between these two.

### The one-take: mosque → park
- One-take video: standing in front of the mosque, then running over to the park — all in one shot. Can be sped up as a transition to show how close these two worlds are.
- In the same shot, the moment of arrival at the park: people started shouting — why are you filming? put the camera down. They were screaming. Kept running through.
- [ADD] This single shot IS the thesis of the video: soul and street, meters apart, one breath. Strong candidate for the structural spine — the video can return to this run as a chapter divider. The shouting at the end of the take is a free, real danger beat inside the same shot; don't cut it off.

### The park — first contact
- Several shots of Africans standing inside the park, sitting at benches, dealing.
- Scene: camera standing, framing set — a man walks straight up into the frame and asks: what is this camera? what for? Got interrupted.
- His own script idea (keep): mention that it's difficult to film there — even when I set up my camera, someone walks into the frame and asks why I'm filming.
- A few shots from the entry of the park.

### Fixpunkt: the trash can
- Met someone from Fixpunkt. She told him, agitated, that this trash can should be closed — but somehow it's open.
- The point: it's not a regular trash can. It contains injection materials — used cans, basically, still with drugs inside them. It was open, so children could hurt themselves if something fell out.
- Filmed the Fixpunkt person explaining what it is, plus the Spritzen (syringes) in the Eimer/bucket picked out of the trash.
- [ADD] Strongest public-interest beat of Day 1: it's not about the addicts, it's about the CHILDREN at risk — unarguable, unpolitical, and it protects the video against "he's just filming misery." Give it room.

### The €50,000 toilet
- Public toilet at Kottbusser Tor: costs over fifty thousand euros per year to maintain. People inject themselves inside.
- Filmed standing in front of it, mentioning that people are inside right now, at this moment.

## DAY 2 — GÖRLITZER PARK

### The motorbike opening
- Driving through the city on a motorbike, filming himself driving through Berlin.
- Arrival at the park: hard brake directly in front of the entry — nice scene.
- Standing up from the bike, line delivered: this is the entry of the park, people already doing business as you see — let's get out of here, because it's not easy to film here.
- [ADD] Clean, energetic cold-open alternative if the bottle throw doesn't open the video (see Hook Candidates below). Also works as the Day-2 chapter start.

### Approaches and refusals
- Multiple scenes recording himself after trying to approach someone. When approached, they said: no, I don't want to be filmed. Some became aggressive. Some said: I'll answer your question, but I don't want to be in front of the camera.
- [ADD] These refusal moments are content, not failure — they prove the difficulty and honesty (nothing staged). One or two of these, quick, establish why the interview with Kamara is so valuable: after all the refusals, ONE man talks.
- Production note for Michael: blur the ones who refused — same practice as Kamara's face.

### The consumption zone
- Filmed the area where people consume drugs with no legal consequences inside the park — but outside the park, you get trouble for taking drugs.
- While saying this, right in front of him, someone was sniffing something.
- Fixpunkt even builds buses to make it easier/safer for the addicts to inject themselves. Filmed these buses.
- [ADD] The inside/outside-the-park paradox is a strong, contained observation — state it exactly like that, no bigger claim needed. The buses visual carries the rest.

### The people
- Met a Moroccan and an Algerian inside the park.
- Met [CHECK: "american" — likely Cameroonian? he freestyled in French] and a Nigerian. One did a spontaneous freestyle in French. The other tried to gift hashish for free — kindly refused.
- One African cutting the hair of another (Ghana) — on a rock, with a shaving machine he brought.
- Another one cooking rice and food for people right there — African street food.
- His observation (keep): at first there's a distance, it feels closed off — but as soon as there's a little bit of trust, it's like sitting at a restaurant. Very lively.
- Met many crazy people there — a lot of people who lost their minds inside the park.
- [ADD] The barber + the street-food cook are the humanity beats — the park as a functioning parallel world, not just misery. This is the "seeing the invisible" layer: life, economy, dignity inside the place everyone writes off. These shots + Kamara = the heart. The freestyle is the joy beat — use it.

### The storage in the bushes
- Filmed someone inside the bushes. Not consuming — hiding the stuff. The storage of the whole park is inside the trees and inside the bushes.
- Line idea (his, keep): if you wonder why people are walking into the trees — that's why.
- [ADD] Great reveal beat: set up the mystery (people walking into trees), pay it off. Mini open-loop inside the video.

### The marijuana packets
- Someone showed him marijuana, two packets. Took them in his hand and filmed them.
- His own framing (keep): which, by the way, is theoretically legal in Germany under certain circumstances. But of course I don't consume or have anything to do with it.

### The bottle throw
- When leaving: most people were kind, but one got aggressive. Why are you filming? Told him he's not filming him — he still started screaming.
- When turning his back to leave the park, the man threw a glass bottle in his direction. The whole process is on film — you HEAR the glass smash on the ground behind him. Then his line on camera: this guy just threw a bottle at me.
- His own note (keep): this could be a nice entry, a nice hook — it communicates real danger, even though the situation was not that dangerous or serious.
- [ADD] Agreed — hook candidate #1. His own honesty note is the guardrail: use the moment exactly as it happened (bottle, smash, reaction), don't escalate it in VO beyond what it was. The SOUND of the glass is the hook — let the audio lead, even half a second on black.
